# Code of conduct

Pharos welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code applies to project
issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Participating

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate. Make room for questions and different perspectives.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people. Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Ask before sharing someone else's personal information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, accept reasonable feedback, and help resolve disagreements.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a repository maintainer privately using the contact method on their public profile.
If no private contact method is listed, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel;
do not include incident details or identifying information in that issue. For abuse on GitHub,
you may also use GitHub's built-in reporting tools.

Include what happened, where it happened, and relevant links or evidence in the private report.
Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share information only as needed to investigate
and respond. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, issue a warning, restrict participation
temporarily, or ban participants, depending on the severity and recurrence of the behavior.
They should explain the reason to the affected participant when practical. A participant may
request a review privately; a maintainer involved in the incident should not review that report.
